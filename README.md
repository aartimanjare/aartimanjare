## 🚀 Major projects

Most of my work is in private client repositories, so the projects below are described without client names or code. I'm happy to walk through the details in an interview.

### Procure-to-pay and GST compliance suite · *Financial services, 2026*
A custom Frappe app on ERPNext v16 covering the full purchase cycle for a large financial-services group.
- **Requisition and purchase controls:** requisition (BRN) flows, vendor comparison, rate-history and mismatch alerts, and PO / invoice limits against approved budgets.
- **Indian GST rules:** Place of Supply, ITC eligibility, HSN-level ineligibility, ISD, reverse charge and automated ITC reversal for time-barred invoices.
- **Compliance:** MSME payment compliance, supplier onboarding and TDS category mapping.
- **Delivery:** 31 pull requests with patches, unit and regression tests, and go-live documentation.

### Field service and subscription management · *Home-appliance rental and service, 2026*
- **Plans and maintenance:** membership and lump-sum plans, with automated maintenance scheduling and rescheduling.
- **Technician app backend:** REST APIs for tasks, signatures, and parts used or exchanged, with serial-number tracking.
- **Inventory:** stock issue created automatically when a task is completed.
- **Integrations and alerts:** sync with the IoT device platform, role-based workflows and real-time technician notifications.

### Payment gateway integration app (Paystack and PalmPay) · *Reusable app, 2025–26*
- **Reusable design:** one client-agnostic Frappe app that lets customers pay Sales Invoices, Sales Orders or advances online.
- **Payment safety:** signature-verified webhooks (HMAC-SHA512 / RSA), verification APIs and a scheduled reconciliation job, with transaction locking so no payment is missed or posted twice.
- **Payment Entries:** created automatically and allocated by payment terms.

### Headless CMS and knowledge platform · *Non-profit organisation, 2024–26*
- **Multi-site backend:** one Frappe backend serving several public websites through APIs.
- **Content model:** publications, datasets, podcasts, infographics, impact stories and experts, in Hindi and English.
- **Search and charts:** full-text search with filters, pagination and counts, plus APIs that serve chart data for dashboards.

### Jewellery manufacturing and inventory · *Manufacturing, 2024–25*
- **Production tracking:** melting lots, stone assortment, loss accounting and manufacturing workflows.
- **Output:** stock reports and print formats for the production floor.

### Headless e-commerce and B2B digital catalogues · *Retail and distribution, 2023–25*
- **Storefront APIs:** custom ERPNext-backed APIs for cart, tax-aware pricing, product bundles, stock availability and user registration.
- **Auto parts:** catalogues searchable by vehicle.

### More
